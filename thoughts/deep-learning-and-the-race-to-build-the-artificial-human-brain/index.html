Deep Learning and the Race to Build the Artificial Human Brain

Hashem AlyAugust 19, 20154 min read

Deep Learning is a subject many tech companies and universities such as Google, Facebook and Stanford are currently focusing on intensely. The idea behind deep learning is setting up machines to work like the human brain. The Human Brain is made up of millions upon millions of neurons that transfer messages around the brain and body. With deep learning scientists are creating artificial neurons to create a machine that learns without the aid of humans, very similar to the brain.

Andrew Ng is a former Stanford professor hired by Google in 2010 to lead their deep learning efforts. Andrew was placed within Google X which is a secret division within Google, lead by Google cofounder Sergey Brin, that creates ’Science Fiction sounding solutions’. Projects within Google X include self-driving cars, augmented reality glasses, contact lenses that monitor glucose levels in tears, internet service via Balloons in the stratosphere and Google Brain formerly known as the Google Deep Learning Project.

Google Brain was set up in a way very much similar to the human brain. It is a neural network that uses unsupervised learning. In other words a machine that can understand images and voices without human input. The Google Brain was set up in a way to let the machines learn on their own.

Recently 10 million images of cats were displayed to the machine by watching Youtube videos and the result was that the machine can now detect a cat when it sees one. However according to Andrew Ng the project lead the accuracy is currently at 70%.

Baidu which is the ’Google of China’ as many call them have recently hired Andrew Ng from Google to lead their own AI Project. The objective is to create machines that can learn about the world around them on their own. Ng recently sat with MIT Tech and this is what he had to say about the subject.

At Stanford—inspired by the Google Brain experiment—you developed an even larger neural network. Will bigger “brains” automatically be smarter?

For the scale of the challenge we’re looking at—human level performance—it’s very clear that for a small neural network there’s no hope. State-of-the-art ones have hundreds of millions of connections. You can do a lot with that; recognize a lot of objects, for example.

[But] it does not seem to be as simple as just making the neural network much bigger. The Google Brain result was built on a huge distributed system with a lot of CPU cores [16,000]. We found that if you put a lot of GPUs [specialized graphics processors] together we could make a much bigger neural network—10 billion nodes, with 16 machines instead of 1,000.

We used that same benchmark [images from YouTube videos] that the Google team did. But even though we could train a much larger neural net, we didn’t necessarily get a better cat detector. Right now we can run neural networks that are larger than we know what to do with.

[At the Baidu lab] we want to build a framework to run big enough experiments to test out all variations in algorithms that might universally improve performance.

Facebook is another company that has its own AI project called DeepFace. They were able to create a facial processing software that can detect human faces almost at human standard. If you show a human two pictures of the same person, the human will know that they are the same person 97.53 percent of the time. When the same test was done using DeepFace the result was 97.25 percent.

DeepFace is a facial verification software which means it recognizes that two images show the same face and not a facial recognition software which is linking a face to a name. However the same techniques can be applied to create facial recognition, which would seem more useful for Facebook as they could use that for tagging photos automatically for example.

DeepFace uses 3D modeling to rotate the face to make it face the camera. Which means that the person in the picture may be facing the camera in one picture and not facing the camera in another and DeepFace will still guess the person correctly.

Stanford is another institution involved in the race to create the artificial brain. Recently a group of bioengineers created a circuit board that is set up like the human brain. It works 9000 times faster than a normal PC and uses significantly less power.

However the human brain has 80,000 times more neurons than the circuit board created but uses only 3 times the power. Which means that ’neurogrid’ the name of the circuit board has a long way to go in terms of efficiency.

The Stanford bioengineers believe that there are a lot of positive implications to what they have created. They believe both a better understanding of the human brain and huge advancements in robotics can be achieved from their progress in Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning.

“a chip as fast and efficient as the human brain could drive prosthetic limbs with the speed and complexity of our own actions.”

These are just a couple of implementation of Deep Learning however as companies and institutions such as Google, Facebook, Baidu and Stanford make progress in deep learning and Artificial Intelligence it will be interesting to see how they will implement these experiments and efforts in commercial products and services.
